Do this before your interview

  1. Research the company — products, recent news, mission, and 2–3 competitors. You'll need this for the "why us" and competitor questions.
  2. Build your own real examples for strengths, weaknesses, leadership, teamwork, and pressure. Generic answers without a story fall flat.
  3. Know your number — check Glassdoor, AmbitionBox, or Naukri for your role and location before they ask about salary.
  4. Practice out loud — record yourself or rehearse with a friend. Delivery carries as much weight as content.
  5. Have 2–3 questions ready to ask them. "No questions" reads as disinterest.

Tip: share your actual branch of study, experience, target role, and target company, and every answer above can be sharpened to fit you specifically.
